Identity specification

The complete written brief behind the board — strategy, mark rationale, construction rules, palette, typography, and voice. Executable as-is by any design or image tool.

01 · Brand strategy

Category — medical-research professional education (LMS for clinical trial design, biostatistics, systematic reviews, medical AI, manuscript writing, research ethics).

Audience — clinical researchers, biostatisticians, PhD students, GCP & regulatory staff — professionals whose credibility depends on methodological rigor.

Emotional promise — precision, credibility, mastery. The brand must feel like the *peer-reviewed journal of learning*: trusted, exact, calm.

Core metaphor — the clinical protocol: every course is a protocol; every lesson a controlled step; every certificate an audited result. Evidence in, conclusion out.

What to avoid — generic edtech gradients, playful mascots, gamification noise, "AI-glow" purple, dense marketing copy.

02 · Logo — "The Protocol Mark"

  • Monogram + meaning — an open-bowl geometric P built on a strict 8u measurement grid.
  • Negative-space crosshair — the open bowl carries a calibration crosshair: precision as the brand's operating state.
  • Evidence line — a terminal amber rule with an arrowhead: systematic reduction to a conclusion.
  • Constructed, not drawn — stem 4u, bars 2u, bowl 2u; no curves, no gradients; survives 16px favicon to 4m signage.
  • Variants — icon / horizontal lockup / reversed (paper-on-ink) / app tile.

03 · Color system

Ink#0C1210

Teal#1F8A70
Amber#C9962E

One accent carries the system (teal = learning); amber is reserved for certification and conclusions. Teal must never sit on amber; paper text never on paper.

Serif for editorial trust; grotesque for interface; mono for protocol data, labels and the "system voice". Wordmark set in letterspaced caps, never tracked tighter than +0.3em.

05 · Voice & taglines

Primary — "Evidence you can build on."

Campaign — "Build evidence that matters." (site DNA)

Alternates — "Precision in every protocol." · "Research, reduced to method." · "Every mission under control."

Tone: calm, exact, understated. Sentences short; claims specific; no superlatives without a number beside them.

06 · Application & do/don't

Do — keep generous negative space; use the evidence line as the sole animated device (progress bars, loaders, transitions); set protocol/syllabus data in mono; use the mark at 16px minimum.

Don't — rotate or recolor the crosshair; add drop shadows to the mark; place the mark on halftone; mix the teal with rainbow accents; set the wordmark in serif.
