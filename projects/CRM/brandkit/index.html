Identity specification

The complete written brief behind the board — strategy, mark rationale, construction rules, palette, typography, and voice.

01 · Brand strategy

Category — B2B revenue workspace: CRM, pipeline management, deal tracking, forecasting, renewal automation.

Audience — sales leaders, revenue operators, growth teams, account executives — professionals who live in pipelines and close quarters.

Emotional promise — velocity, control, clarity. The brand must feel like the *operational nerve center*: fast, reliable, always moving.

Core metaphor — the revenue loop: every deal is a cycle; every touchpoint a beat; every close a loop completed. Pipeline in, revenue out.

What to avoid — generic SaaS gradients, playful mascots, gamification noise, "AI-glow" purple, dense marketing copy, corporate jargon.

02 · Logo — "The Recurrence Mark"

  • Loop + revenue — an open recurrence arc (product action) combined with vertical revenue bars (currency metaphor).
  • Negative-space chevron — the arc terminus carries a directional arrowhead: velocity and forward motion.
  • Revenue spark — parallel amber bars suggest deal values, currency, and recurring income.
  • Construction — 80r arc, 14w stroke, 7w bars; no curves beyond the arc; survives 16px to 4m.
  • Variants — icon / horizontal lockup / app tile (sky-on-ink) / reversed (ink-on-sky).

05 · Voice & taglines

Primary — "Revenue that recurs."

Campaign — "Close faster. Retain longer."

Alternates — "Every deal, tracked." · "Pipeline intelligence." · "From first touch to closed-won."

Tone: fast, operational, tactical. Sentences short; claims specific; no superlatives without a number beside them.

06 · Application & do/don't

Do — keep generous negative space; use the recurrence arc as the sole animated device (pipeline flows, loaders, transitions); set deal values in mono; use the mark at 16px minimum.

Don't — rotate or recolor the arc; add drop shadows to the mark; place the mark on halftone; mix sky with rainbow accents; set the wordmark in serif.
