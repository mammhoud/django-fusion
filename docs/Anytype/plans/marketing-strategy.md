## 4. Sales Process

```
Lead Capture → Demo → Trial → Onboarding → Support → Expansion
```

| Stage | Activity | Duration | Owner |
|-------|----------|:--------:|-------|
| Lead Capture | Website form, GitHub, events | Ongoing | Marketing |
| Demo | Product demonstration, use case alignment | 1 hour | Sales |
| Trial | Self-hosted trial deployment | 14 days | Customer |
| Onboarding | Setup assistance, training | 1 week | Support |
| Expansion | Feature adoption, upgrade path | Ongoing | Customer success |

---

## 5. Growth Tactics

- **Content Marketing** — Regular technical blog posts and tutorials
- **Open Source Community** — Contributor recognition, GitHub sponsors
- **Referral Program** — User referrals for premium plan credits
- **Partnerships** — Agency partnerships for white-label deployments
- **Educational Discounts** — Special pricing for academic institutions
