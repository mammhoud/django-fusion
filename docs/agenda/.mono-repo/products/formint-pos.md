# Formint POS — Product Description

> **Description:** Arabic-first restaurant operations platform that keeps local service running offline and provides a path from self-hosted Professional operations to managed POS Cloud services.

## Customer problem

Restaurant operators need checkout, kitchen coordination, inventory, customer relationships, and branch visibility without losing service continuity or control of their data.

## Product promise

Formint helps restaurants sell, prepare, manage, and grow from a dependable local workflow. Professional provides offline desktop/PWA operations; POS Cloud adds hosted tenants, branch coordination, analytics, backups, billing, and integrations.

## Product pillars

| Pillar | Customer benefit | Proof to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Service continuity | Continue core work during network interruption | Offline/reconnect pilot results |
| Restaurant depth | KDS, combos, modifiers, extras, recipes, waste, and notes | Workflow and acceptance tests |
| Arabic-first experience | RTL, bilingual menus, local invoice presentation | Language and usability review |
| Branch control | Publish, compare, reconcile, and audit branches | Multi-branch pilot |
| Extensibility | Versioned API and partner integrations | External integration test |

## Pricing hypothesis

- Community: free/self-hosted core.
- Formint Professional: self-hosted license or annual support plan; validate the proposed range with pilots.
- POS Cloud: subscription by branch, usage, and support level.

Pricing, compliance, performance, and revenue claims remain hypotheses until the evidence is recorded in the claims register.
