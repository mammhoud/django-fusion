# Social Networks & Community Plan

> **Description:** Channel strategy for building trust, education, product discovery, feedback, partnerships, and community participation.

## Channel roles

| Channel | Role | Content | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| GitHub | Developer trust and contribution | Releases, issues, architecture, examples | Contributors and qualified adopters |
| LinkedIn | B2B awareness and partnerships | Operator outcomes, pilots, team stories | Qualified conversations |
| YouTube | Product education | Demos, setup, KDS, offline workflows | Watch completion and demo requests |
| Email | Nurture and onboarding | Guides, release notes, trial reminders | Activation and retention |
| Discord/community | Support and feedback | Help, discussions, beta feedback | Resolved questions and active members |
| Local networks/events | Regional trust | Arabic demos, restaurant workshops, partners | Pilot referrals |

## Content method

Teach first: show a restaurant problem, demonstrate the workflow, state the current release status, provide proof or limitation, and offer one clear next action.

## Governance

Each channel has an owner, moderation rules, response time, consent policy, content calendar, and escalation path. Connect campaign content to a Product, Plan, Team, and claim-evidence record.
