# User Guide Overview

Welcome to the VResume User Guide. This section will help you understand how to manage your professional portfolio using the Wagtail CMS.

## 📝 Content Management

VResume is designed around a "Single Source of Truth" philosophy. You manage your data once in the CMS, and it is automatically formatted and displayed across the site.

### Key Management Areas:

- **[Portfolio Management](portfolio.md)**: 
    Manage your projects, case studies, and technical skills. Learn how to tag projects by technology and feature them on your homepage.
- **[Blog Management](blog.md)**: 
    Write articles using Wagtail's rich text editor. Organize posts with tags and reusable content snippets (like testimonials or code blocks).
- **[Newsletter Management](newsletter.md)**: 
    Monitor your growing subscriber list. View subscriber details and manage their active status.
- **[Analytics & Interactions](analytics.md)**: 
    Review contact form submissions, track visitor engagement, and monitor site health.

## 🛠️ Site-Wide Configuration (Structa Settings)

Under the **Settings** menu in the Wagtail sidebar, you can find the **VResume Settings**:

### 1. Personal Branding
- **Full Name & Job Title**: These are used in the site header, footer, and SEO titles.
- **Profile Avatar**: Your professional photo used in the "About" and "Hero" sections.
- **Branding Logos**: Upload custom logos for the navigation bar and footers.

### 2. Contact & Social
- **Global Social Links**: A centralized place to manage links to LinkedIn, GitHub, X (Twitter), etc.
- **Contact Information**: Your public email, phone, and physical location.
- **Map Integration**: Embed a Google Maps URL for your contact page.

### 3. SEO & Meta
- **Global Metadata**: Set the default site description and keywords.
- **Social Sharing**: Configure how your site looks when shared on LinkedIn or Twitter.
