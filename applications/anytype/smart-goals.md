# SMART Goals   
SMART goals are intended to focus attention and resources on what is most important so that you can be successful in achieving your priorities.    
| **S**   <br> |   Specific   <br> |                   What will be accomplished? <br>What actions will you take?   <br> |
|:-------------|:------------------|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **M**   <br> | Measurable   <br> |                     What data will measure the goal? <br>How much? How well?   <br> |
| **A**   <br> | Achievable   <br> |      Is the goal doable? <br>Do you have the necessary skills and resources?   <br> |
| **R**   <br> |   Relevant   <br> | How does the goal align with broader goals? <br>Why is the result important?   <br> |
| **T**   <br> | Time-Bound   <br> |                           What is the time frame for accomplishing the goal?   <br> |

*Source: [University of California](https://ucop.edu/)*
